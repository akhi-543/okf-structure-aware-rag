This department inspires customers to invest in their living spaces through products that transform homes and outdoor areas into places of beauty and comfort. Seasonal merchandising creates excitement around new possibilities, while displays demonstrate product applications and combinations that spark customer imagination. The team serves both practical needs and aspirational goals, helping customers envision and achieve their home improvement dreams through knowledgeable recommendations. Visual displays that show how products work together and how customers can create attractive spaces help drive traffic and demonstrate merchandise possibilities beyond basic function.

## Notes

This department creates joy and possibilities for play, learning, and family entertainment across childhood years and developmental stages. Merchandising strategy organizes by age and interest, displaying products that spark imagination and support developmental needs appropriately. The team balances expertise about educational value with enthusiasm for pure fun, helping customers select toys that genuinely delight children. Parents appreciate guidance about age-appropriate selection and value recommendations that help them choose toys that grow with children and maintain interest over time.

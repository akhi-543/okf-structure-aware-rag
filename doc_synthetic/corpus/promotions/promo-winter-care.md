# Winter Care

This promotion applies to the [Pharmacy](/departments/dept-pharmacy.md) department.

**Dates:** 2025-12-01 to 2025-12-31

**Discount:** 12% off

Supercenter and neighborhood stores are valid for Winter Care.

## Notes

This promotion offers value on products that customers depend on throughout the year during different times. Timing aligns with genuine customer needs while messaging emphasizes preparation and care. The campaign combines value with accessibility, helping customers manage their needs without financial concern. Education about products and their use helps customers understand value and supports repeat purchases throughout the promotion period.

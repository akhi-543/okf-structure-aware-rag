# Fresh Friday

This promotion applies to the [Grocery](/departments/dept-grocery.md) department.

**Dates:** 2025-01-03 to 2025-12-26

**Discount:** 10% off

Fresh Friday is available at supercenter, neighborhood, and express formats.

## Notes

This promotion builds loyalty through consistent, reliable value on products that customers purchase regularly throughout the year. Long timing emphasizes everyday value that matters to budget-conscious households making routine shopping decisions. The campaign combines accessibility with genuine value, creating reasons for customers to shop frequently and build lasting shopping habits. Consistent promotion reinforces customer habit and expectation around shopping at the store for regular needs and purchases.

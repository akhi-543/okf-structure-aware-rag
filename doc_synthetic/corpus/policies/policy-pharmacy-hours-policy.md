# Pharmacy Hours Policy

The pharmacy operates 10:00-18:00 on Sundays under this policy.
Monday through Friday, pharmacy hours are 08:00-21:00.

## Notes

This policy balances customer convenience with operational sustainability, ensuring services align with store hours and community needs. Consistent availability builds customer confidence that customers can access what they need when they need it. Fair terms demonstrate our commitment to service access and customer care for all community members. Flexibility reflects understanding of modern schedules and ensures access for busy families.

# Returns Policy

The Returns Policy provides 15 days for electronics returns.
A receipt must be presented to return items under this policy.
Under the Returns Policy, most items can be returned within 30 days of purchase.

## Notes

This policy reflects our commitment to customer confidence through fair, transparent terms that honor both shopper needs and business integrity. Clear guidelines and straightforward requirements demonstrate respect for customer concerns while building trust in merchandise quality. Consistent application builds confidence that customers are valued and protected when they shop with us. Customer service orientation ensures people feel heard and supported, building long-term loyalty through fair treatment.

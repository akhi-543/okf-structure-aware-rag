# Fuel Rewards Policy

The Fuel Rewards Policy offers 10 cents off per gallon.
Customers must spend $100 to qualify for benefits.

## Notes

This policy rewards loyal shoppers with value on services available to those who shop regularly. Transparent calculation and straightforward earning build confidence that customers receive fair treatment and genuine value from loyalty. The program reinforces our appreciation for customer loyalty and encourages repeat shopping that benefits both stores and customers. Easy participation and clear communication about rewards make customers feel valued and motivated to continue shopping.

## Notes

The West region embraces innovation and adaptability in meeting the diverse customer expectations of different store locations and community types. Store operations emphasize flexibility in merchandising strategies, thoughtful store layouts that guide customers through departments logically, and significant investments in team training that prepare associates to handle a wide range of customer interactions effectively. The region's team culture values problem-solving, continuous improvement, and creating an inviting environment where customers feel genuinely cared for and understood. Store managers maintain open communication with regional leadership, enabling quick response to local opportunities and ensuring that every location contributes meaningfully to regional success and customer satisfaction.

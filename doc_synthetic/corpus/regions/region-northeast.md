## Notes

The Northeast region thrives with a dedicated team committed to exceptional customer service and operational excellence. Store teams work closely together, sharing best practices for visual merchandising, community engagement, and inventory management. The region's success comes from strong collaboration between management and front-line staff, combined with a genuine focus on keeping stores clean, well-organized, and responsive to customer needs. This collaborative spirit creates a culture where experienced team members mentor newer associates and where good ideas flow freely across the region.

# Brightmart Magnolia Row

This store is situated in Savannah, GA.

Brightmart Magnolia Row is a supercenter format store.

Brightmart Magnolia Row opened its doors in 2008.

Brightmart Magnolia Row spans 176,000 square feet.

Pharmacy services are available on-site.

Customers can fill up at the fuel station.

This location is open to customers.
